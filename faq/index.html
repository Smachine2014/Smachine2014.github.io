FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about class suitability, beginner pathways, training safety, and fees at North Shore Judo.

Home / FAQ

Do I need to be fit before starting judo?

No. Our beginner classes are designed to help you build fitness progressively regardless of your starting point.

Can beginners join adult classes?

Yes. Most adult members started as complete beginners and learn from the ground up.

What age can kids start judo?

Children can start from age 4 in our Pee Wees programme in Takapuna.

How much do classes cost?

Pee Wees are $36/month. Juniors and Seniors are $73/month. Your first class is always free!

Where is the dojo located?

34 Barrys Point Road, Takapuna, Auckland 0622, with easy access for North Shore families.

Is judo safe for kids and adults?

Yes. Coaches teach breakfalls, controlled technique, and age-appropriate progressions to prioritise safety.
