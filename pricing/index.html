How Judo Membership Works

All memberships at North Shore Judo are billed monthly on a three-month term. You don't need to worry about payments at all. This keeps pricing straightforward — no hidden fees, no long-term lock-in contracts. If your circumstances change, talk to Simon and we will work something out.

Your first class is always free so you get a chance to experience the training environment, meet the coaches, and decide whether judo is right for you or your child before making any financial commitment.

Fees cover full access to your class sessions, coaching, use of the fully matted dojo, gym, kitchen etc and support from senior members during training. Judo New Zealand affiliation and grading fees are separate and managed through the national federation.
