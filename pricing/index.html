How Judo Membership Works

If your circumstances change, talk to Simon and we will work something out.

Your first class is always free so you get a chance to experience the training environment, meet the coaches, and decide whether judo is right for you or your child before making any financial commitment.

Fees cover full access to your class sessions, coaching, use of the fully matted training space, and support from senior members during training. Judo New Zealand affiliation and grading fees are separate and managed through the national federation.

The North Shore Judo Academy was founded in 1969 by Sensei Ron Jones (5th dan) and has shared a love of Judo with thousands of players in that time, including the current coaching team.
