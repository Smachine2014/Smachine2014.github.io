Choose a Class and Get Started

Whether you are a parent researching martial arts for your child, or an adult considering judo for the first time, these guides cover the questions people ask most before joining a club. Each guide is written with Auckland's North Shore context in mind — including what to wear, how classes are structured, and what makes judo different from other martial arts.

Topics include how judo compares to karate and taekwondo, what a typical training session looks like, how belt progression works in New Zealand, and what equipment you need to get started.

We update these guides regularly based on questions new members ask during their first weeks at the dojo. If something is not covered here, get in touch and we will be happy to help.

The North Shore Judo Academy was founded in 1969 by Sensei Ron Jones (5th dan) and has shared a love of Judo with thousands of players in that time, including the current coaching team.
