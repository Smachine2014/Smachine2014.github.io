Meet the Team

Our coaches blend technical judo instruction with clear communication and strong safety standards. Every class is structured to help beginners feel welcome while giving advanced members room to progress.

Senior high-belt members regularly support class drills and partner work, sharing practical experience from different training and competition backgrounds.

Simon Greenhill

Head Coach. Started judo at age five and recognised by Judo New Zealand as a National Club Coach in 2006.

Doug

Coach supporting technical improvement, class progression, and disciplined training habits across all levels.

Coaching Approach

Safety first, clear progressions, and practical instruction for kids judo, youth judo, and adult judo.

Community Focus

Building a friendly, inclusive dojo culture where every member can thrive and learn from coaches and experienced high-belt peers.

Our coaching team serves members from Takapuna, Milford, Devonport, Glenfield, Wairau, Birkenhead, Birkdale, and Albany.

Getting started: sign up online, then come to your preferred class and introduce yourself to Simon.
