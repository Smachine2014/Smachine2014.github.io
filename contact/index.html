What to Expect When You Visit

When you arrive at the dojo for the first time, head to 88 College Road, Northcote. We share the building with Cornerstone BJJ, behind Transformation Academy. Follow the yellow route in the building guide above from the car park. Arrive around 10 minutes early so you can meet coach Simon and our friendly members before your session begins.

You don't need a judo uniform (gi) for your first class — comfortable clothing like tracksuit pants and a t-shirt is fine. We train on a fully matted surface, and all sessions are supervised by experienced coaches and senior members.

If you are unsure which class to attend, email or call us and we will help you choose the right session based on age and experience level. Parents are welcome to stay and watch during kids and junior classes.

The North Shore Judo Academy was founded in 1969 by Sensei Ron Jones (5th dan) and has shared a love of Judo with thousands of players in that time, including the current coaching team.
