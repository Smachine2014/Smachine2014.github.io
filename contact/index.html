What to Expect When You Visit

When you arrive at the dojo for the first time, head to the main entrance at 34 Barrys Point Road, Takapuna. There is free parking directly outside the building. Arrive around 10 minutes early so you can meet coach Simon and other friendly before your session begins.

You don't need a judo uniform (gi) for your first class — comfortable clothing like tracksuit pants and a t-shirt is fine. We train on a fully matted, competition-size surface, and all sessions are supervised by experienced coaches and senior members.

If you are unsure which class to attend, email or call us and we will help you choose the right session based on age and experience level. Parents are welcome to stay and watch during kids and junior classes.
