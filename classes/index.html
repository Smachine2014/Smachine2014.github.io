Start with a Free Class

Classes are currently full until the end of the year. Please still use the signup buttons below to register your interest for the next available places.

If you are comparing martial arts classes in Auckland, start with one free judo session and see how our coaching works in person. We regularly welcome families and adults from Takapuna, Milford, Devonport, Glenfield, Wairau, Birkenhead, and Albany.

Our regular timetable follows the school term, so there are usually no classes during school holidays.

Getting started: sign up online to join the waitlist, and we will contact you when places open up.

Pee Wees intake note: Pee Wees intake is currently limited to the start of Terms 1 and 3 due to class spaces. For availability outside intake periods, contact info@northshorejudo.co.nz.
