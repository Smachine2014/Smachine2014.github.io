Why Adults Choose Judo

Adult judo offers practical self-defence awareness, full-body conditioning, and structured technical learning. You do not need previous martial arts experience to join. Our coaches scale instruction to your level and goals.

  • Beginner-friendly onboarding and clear progression.
  • Technical throws, groundwork, and controlled sparring.
  • Excellent training for strength, mobility, and confidence.
  • Supportive community atmosphere in Takapuna.

Class times: Tuesday & Thursday 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM
