How Junior Classes Work

Junior students learn core throwing, groundwork, and breakfall skills while improving discipline and athletic confidence. Classes are designed for both beginners and returning judoka.

High-belt members regularly partner with juniors during technical drills, which helps students learn timing, balance, and etiquette from people with strong practical experience.

  • Structured lesson progression and safe sparring drills.
  • Coaching support for belt progression and technique refinement.
  • Competition pathway for students who want to compete.
  • Inclusive and respectful training culture.
