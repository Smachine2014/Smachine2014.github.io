What happens on the mat

Junior students learn core throwing, groundwork and breakfall skills while improving discipline and athletic confidence. Classes are designed for both beginners and returning judoka, with time to learn a technique and practise it with a partner.

High-belt members regularly partner with juniors during technical drills. That gives students a chance to learn timing, balance and etiquette from people with strong practical experience.

  • Structured lessons and controlled sparring drills.
  • Coaching support for belt progression and refining technique.
  • Opportunities for students who want to compete.
  • A respectful training environment where beginners can ask questions.

Judo for North Shore families

Families from Takapuna, Milford, Devonport, Albany, Glenfield, Wairau, Birkenhead and Birkdale choose junior judo to help children build resilience, focus and confidence through practical martial arts training.

For ages 7–14, the junior class is the next step after Pee Wees and a starting point for children who have never tried judo. Your child can try a free class to see how it feels.
