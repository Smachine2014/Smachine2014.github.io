What children learn in Pee Wees

Our Pee Wees judo programme introduces children to movement, listening skills and respectful behaviour through age-appropriate drills. Each session focuses on balance, falling safely and confidence in a positive group environment.

Alongside coach-led instruction, experienced high-belt members help demonstrate technique in a calm and beginner-friendly way, so children learn with clear examples from day one.

  • A first experience of judo for ages 4–6, with no previous martial arts experience needed.
  • Practice in coordination, listening and focus.
  • Teamwork and respect for the people they train with.
  • Age-appropriate coaching in a fully matted dojo.

Is this the right class for your child?

If you are searching for kids martial arts near Takapuna or in Northcote, this class is a place to build confidence, enjoy healthy movement and get comfortable on the mat before moving into junior judo.

Families travel from Milford, Devonport, Glenfield, Wairau, Birkenhead, Birkdale and Albany for our beginner judo programme on Auckland’s North Shore. The first class is free, so your child can try a session before you decide.
