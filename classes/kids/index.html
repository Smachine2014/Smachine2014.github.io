What Kids Learn in Pee Wees Judo

Our Pee Wees judo programme introduces children to movement, listening skills, and respectful behavior through age-appropriate drills. Each session focuses on balance, falling safely, and confidence in a positive group environment.

  • Designed for ages 4-6 and complete beginners.
  • Builds coordination, confidence, and focus.
  • Encourages teamwork and respectful behavior.
  • Safe coaching methods in a fully matted dojo.
