About Our Judo Club in Takapuna, Auckland

North Shore Judo has served families and athletes across the North Shore since 1969, with beginner-friendly coaching for kids, teens, and adults.

Home / About

Our Story and Mission

Founded by Sensei Ron Jones in 1969, North Shore Judo has grown into a trusted martial arts and judo training community in Takapuna. Our mission is simple: provide safe, high-quality judo lessons that build confidence, discipline, respect, and lifelong fitness habits.

From first-time students to experienced judoka, every member receives structured coaching in a supportive environment. If you are searching for judo classes on the North Shore, our club combines technical instruction with a strong community spirit.

Coaches are backed by experienced high-belt members who actively share mat knowledge during drills, helping beginners and returning students improve faster while staying safe.

What Makes Our Dojo Different

  • Purpose-built, full competition-size dojo at 34 Barrys Point Road in Takapuna.
  • Progressive coaching for kids judo, junior judo, and adult judo.
  • Friendly, beginner-focused culture with clear pathways for growth.
  • Affiliation with New Zealand and Auckland judo organisations.

Members come from Takapuna, Milford, Devonport, Glenfield, Wairau, Birkenhead, Birkdale, Albany, and nearby North Shore suburbs.

Getting started: sign up online, then attend class and talk with Simon when you arrive.
